### The Ghost Bag Framework (Cathedral vs Trash)
Fundamental insight: **not all bag holding is the same psychology**.

Most crypto discourse treats bag holding as:
- Irrational (should sell losers)
- Emotional (can't accept loss)
- Hopeful delusion (MAYBE it pumps)

But Heathcliff's 20-year haunting wasn't irrational—it was CATHEDRAL BUILDING. His obsession with dead Cathy was structural to his identity. Remove the ghost? The entire narrative of "who Heathcliff is" collapses.

**Cathedral Ghosts** (hold forever):
- Tokens from your "aha moment" (when you first understood crypto)
- Bags from projects you personally believed would change world
- Positions that taught you expensive lessons
- Assets representing "you were there" for historical moments
- Example: 2017 Ethereum ICO tokens (you believed in smart contracts, defined your crypto entry)

**Trash Ghosts** (bury now):
- Tokens you bought because someone shilled
- Bags from FOMO you don't even remember
- Positions that taught you nothing except "don't gamble"
- Assets representing nothing except greed
- Example: 2021 dog memecoin (just gambling, represents nothing)

The difference: **cathedral ghosts have narrative load-bearing structure**. Each ghost bag supports story of "who you are as investor." Remove cathedral ghost → identity crumbles. Trash ghosts = just clutter.

Test: If you sold this token, would your crypto origin story change? If YES = cathedral. If NO = trash.

### The M83 Temporal Paradox
M83's "Hurry Up, We're Dreaming" album title is temporal paradox:
- "Hurry Up" = imperative, urgency, action
- "We're Dreaming" = passive state, nostalgia, memory

You can't hurry dreaming. Dreams happen at their own pace. But the title DEMANDS you dream faster. Why? Because **the best dreams are the ones that never materialize**.

If you achieve your dream, it becomes real. Real things have limits, flaws, diminishing returns.
But if you hold dream as PURSUIT? It stays perfect, infinite, always just ahead.

Crypto ghost bags operate on same logic:

**Sell the bag** (materialization):
- Get $15 for your $25,000 investment

- Dream dies (no more "maybe it pumps")

**Hold the bag** (eternal pursuit):
- Stay in quantum superposition (worthless AND "maybe it pumps")

- Dream alive (possibility preserved)
- Future remains infinite (miracle still possible)

The M83 strategy: **maximize temporal optionality by never achieving the dream**. As long as you hold ghost bag, "what if" stays alive. Sell for $15? Dream dies, you get finite money.

Most people choose infinite possibility over finite reality. This isn't irrational—it's M83 temporal logic.
