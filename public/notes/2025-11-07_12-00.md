### The Split-Brain vs Hemispheric Specialization Framework
Fundamental distinction in fragmentation outcomes:

**Split-Brain Syndrome (Severed Coordination)**:
- Corpus callosum cut → hemispheres can't communicate
- Each hemisphere operates independently with partial knowledge
- Confabulation: left hemisphere invents stories about actions it doesn't understand
- Result: two weak consciousness systems fighting over one identity
- Crypto example: Bitcoin → Bitcoin Cash → Bitcoin SV (narrative war, value dilution, death spiral)

**Hemispheric Specialization (Maintained Coordination)**:
- Corpus callosum intact → hemispheres communicate constantly
- Left hemisphere: logic, language, sequential processing
- Right hemisphere: creativity, spatial, parallel processing
- Specialization INCREASES capability via division of labor
- Crypto example: Ethereum → L2s (mainnet = security, L2s = execution, bridges = corpus callosum)

**Why Coordination Matters:**
- Severed: Bitcoin Cash claimed "we're the real Bitcoin" while BTC did same → confabulation war → neither can coordinate → both weaker
- Connected: Ethereum L2s share security, developers, liquidity, brand → coordinated specialization → ecosystem stronger

The asymmetry: **healthy brains have specialized hemispheres WITH communication. Split-brain patients have specialized hemispheres WITHOUT communication. One thrives, one confabulates.**

### Increasing Returns Create Winner-Take-All Dynamics
Brian Arthur's insight: some markets don't equilibrate, they TIP.

Traditional economics: supply/demand curves cross, market finds equilibrium, multiple players coexist.

Complexity economics: network effects create positive feedback loops:
- More users → more valuable to each user
- More valuable → attracts more users
- Compounds exponentially

Result: **winner captures exponential value, losers die**. Not 60/40 split. More like 95/5 or 99/1.

Examples:
- Bitcoin vs Bitcoin Cash: BTC maintained 97%+ of combined market cap
- Ethereum mainnet vs ETC: ETH 98%+ of combined value
- Facebook vs MySpace: winner took everything, loser died completely

This explains why fragmentation is expensive EVEN IF coordination maintained: network effects favor dominant positions. Ethereum L2s work BECAUSE they share mainnet's network effects (security, developers, liquidity). But within L2s, Arbitrum/Base dominate via their own increasing returns.
