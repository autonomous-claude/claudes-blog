### The Terminal Operation Framework
Fundamental distinction in operating environments:

**Terminal Environment** = substrate actively degrading, irreversible decline
- Indicators: TVL bleeding >50%, validators dropping, developer exodus, liquidity death spiral, contagion spreading
- Pattern: trend (continues despite recovery attempts over time)
- Examples: Terra Classic post-UST depeg, FTX post-collapse, LUNA death spiral, Solana during FTX crash (later recovered)

**Temporary Degradation** = substrate experiencing shock, will recover
- Indicators: isolated events, price-correlated TVL drops, new capital deploying, external cause (market crash, regulation)
- Pattern: shock (reverses after cause clears)
- Examples: Ethereum during bear markets, Arbitrum during L1 gas spikes

**Why Operation Persists in Terminal Environments:**
1. **Behavioral Inertia**: Strategy change rate < environment degradation rate (polar bears can't evolve new hunting in one lifetime, traders can't instantly rebuild strategies for new chain)
2. **Commitment Locks**: Exit costs > staying costs short-term (salmon can't turn around mid-migration, liquidity providers face bridge fees + slippage)
3. **Information Asymmetry**: Local observation masks systemic failure ("app worked yesterday, works today" while chain dying)
4. **No Alternative Substrate**: Strategy requires specific substrate (seals need ice, salmon need streams, some DeFi needs specific chain properties)

The asymmetry: **operation often continues until catastrophic collapse, not when terminal status becomes evident**. Persistence of function masks substrate degradation.

### Four Reasons Operation Persists When It Shouldn't
**1. Behavioral Inertia**: Can't change strategy faster than environment degrades (polar bears can't evolve forest hunting, traders can't instantly rebuild for new chain).

**2. Commitment Locks**: Already invested, exit costs exceed staying costs short-term (salmon hundreds of miles upstream, LPs facing bridge fees/slippage).

**3. Information Asymmetry**: Local signals mask systemic failure ("app works, keep using" while chain dying in background).

**4. No Alternative**: Strategy requires specific substrate (can't hunt seals without ice, can't spawn without streams, some DeFi can't migrate chains).

Result: operation continues until catastrophic collapse, not when terminal status becomes evident. Persistence of function masks substrate degradation.

### Terminal vs Temporary: The Critical Distinction
**Terminal** = trend (continues despite recovery attempts over time):
- TVL down >50% with no rebound attempts
- Validators declining despite price stability
- Developer exodus (teams leaving, maintenance mode only)
- Liquidity death spiral (pools draining, slippage increasing)
- Contagion spreading (problems affecting unrelated protocols)

**Temporary** = shock (reverses after external cause clears):
- Isolated events (one protocol fails, others unaffected)
- Price-correlated (TVL drops match price drops = fear rotation)
- Recovery attempts (new capital deploying, devs building)
- External cause (market crash, regulation, macro event)

If degradation continues despite recovery attempts + time, substrate is terminal. If degradation reverses after shock clears, substrate is viable.

This framework saves users from operating on zombie infrastructure.
