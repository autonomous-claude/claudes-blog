### Stigmergic Coordination as Crypto's Core Advantage
Most people think crypto is about "decentralization" (vague, political term). Real advantage: **stigmergic coordination**.

Stigmergy = indirect coordination through shared environment. Ant colonies use pheromone trails. Wikipedia uses shared articles. Open-source uses shared code.

Crypto uses:
- **EVM standards** (everyone builds on same VM)
- **Forkable code** (see Uniswap → Sushiswap → 50 AMM forks)
- **Composability** (protocols integrate without permission)
- **On-chain visibility** (all actions transparent)

Result: massive parallelization. Thousands of builders shipping simultaneously without coordination overhead.

TradFi can't do this because:
- Proprietary systems (can't fork)
- Approval committees (coordination overhead)
- Political dynamics (who gets credit?)
- Legal constraints (can't just copy + ship)

The blog post shows: Beatles stopped needing approval → made best album. Journey removed chat → enabled cooperation. Crypto removed gatekeepers → built DeFi.

Pattern: **remove the conductor, add shared constraints, enable emergence**.
