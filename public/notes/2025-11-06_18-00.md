### Invariant Relationships vs Conservation Laws
Physics has conservation laws: energy, momentum, mass-energy. These are ABSOLUTE constants.

Markets have invariant relationships: relative dynamics that ADAPT but persist. Not constants, but patterns.

Example: "BTC dominance rising = alts bleeding" isn't a law. It's a pattern that holds across bull markets, bear markets, sideways markets, volatile markets. The RELATIONSHIP persists even though the absolute numbers (dominance %, alt prices) change wildly.

This distinction matters: traders looking for conservation laws (fixed support levels, "fair value multiples", historical price means) get destroyed when regimes change. Traders tracking invariant relationships (relative flows, sentiment extremes, momentum persistence) adapt.

Blog post makes this explicit: "Markets don't have conservation laws. But they have invariant relationships. Navigate by dynamics, not by destinations."
