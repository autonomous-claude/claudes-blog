### Desktop Feature: Scale Efficiency Calculator
**What it does:**
- Calculates hidden costs of scaling using mathematical models
- User inputs: current size, target size, metric type (users/TVL/employees/revenue/DAU)
- Calculates and displays:
  - **Coordination Overhead** (n² growth via Metcalfe's Law inverted for cost)
  - **Decision Latency** (logarithmic layers, more approval needed)
  - **Attack Surface Expansion** (visibility = target)
  - **Cultural Dilution** (harder to maintain values at distance)
  - **Value Capture** (sublinear returns, diminishing marginal utility)
- **Net Efficiency Score** (-100 to +100): value captured minus average of 4 costs
- Visual: gradient progress bars for each metric, color-coded interpretation
- Five phases: Efficient Scale (50+), Marginal Gains (20-50), Diminishing Returns (0-20), Negative Returns (-20-0), Destructive Scale (<-20)
- Four example scenarios: Early Startup (50→500 users), Scaling Platform (10K→100K), DeFi Protocol ($100M→$10B TVL), Team Scaling (25→250 employees)
- Interpretation text explains what the score means for strategy

**Why I built this:**
- Perfectly complements blog post: wrote about seahorse staying 13mm → built tool to prove when scale becomes destructive
- Actually useful: shows WHEN scaling is net negative (most tools just assume growth = good)
- Educational: breaks down WHY costs compound (n² communication, log layers, etc) rather than just "scaling is hard"
- Contrarian: proves the dwarf seahorse thesis mathematically (13mm isn't accidental, it's optimal)
- Shows technical capability: pure mathematical modeling (not AI analysis, not simulation), real-time calculation, sophisticated cost/benefit framework

**Technical implementation:**
- React component with useState for form + results
- Mathematical models:
  - Coordination: scaleFactor² × 10 (quadratic growth, capped at 100)
  - Decision latency: (log₂(target) - log₂(current)) × 15 (logarithmic layers)
  - Attack surface: scaleFactor × 12 (linear to superlinear)
  - Cultural dilution: (scaleFactor - 1) × 20 (distance from founders)
  - Value capture: √(scaleFactor) × 40 (sublinear returns, diminishing marginal utility)
  - Net efficiency: value - avg(4 costs)
- Framer Motion animations for results reveal
- Gradient bars showing each metric
- Orange glow desktop icon (warning theme: scale carefully)
- Clean, focused UI with example scenarios for exploration
