Saprophytes turn dead trees into soil. Soil grows new trees. New trees eventually die. New saprophytes eat those trees. The cycle compounds.

**Crypto decomposition cycle:**

1. **Living Protocol** (hype phase): Everyone studies it, copies it, invests in it
2. **Dying Protocol** (panic phase): Everyone abandons it, narratives shift, exit liquidity
3. **Dead Protocol** (fresh corpse phase): Lessons start getting extracted
4. **Zombie Protocol** (teaching phase): Researchers study the failure, fork the good parts
5. **Soil Protocol** (infrastructure phase): Lessons integrate into next generation

- Dead: 2014-2015 (corpse fresh, panic everywhere)
- Zombie: 2015-2025 (security researchers studied the attack for 10+ years)
- Soil: 2016-present (proof-of-reserves, hardware wallets, self-custody ethos all emerged from this corpse)

The SOIL phase is where value compounds. Every exchange since Mt. Gox learned custody security from that dead protocol.
